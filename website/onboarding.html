How to start

Five simple steps. No app download. Sign up happens entirely inside your chat or call.

Open WhatsApp or call the number

Reach Bunty directly by tapping the WhatsApp button or dialing the phone number. You do not need to install an app or create an online account.

Say hello in your preferred language

Bunty automatically detects whether you speak in Hindi, English, Hinglish, or Gujarati from your first few words. There are no language settings to configure.

Confirm your one-time code

A one-time security code is sent to your phone number to verify that you are the guardian of your assistant.

Provide your name and consent

Tell Bunty what to call you. Bunty records your explicit consent for memory and audio processing with a precise timestamp. You can revoke this anytime.

Answer one light introductory question

Bunty asks a quick preference question, such as how you like to receive reminders, and is immediately ready to help with your first task.

Nothing is typed on this website

All registration and interaction happens exclusively within your WhatsApp chat or your normal phone call. To begin right now, tap the button below.
