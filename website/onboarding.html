How to start

Join through a normal call or WhatsApp message. Nothing is typed on this website.

02

Five short steps.

  1. Open the call or chat

    Use the Bunty phone number or WhatsApp link when they are published. No app download is needed.

  2. Start in your language

    Bunty is designed to pick up Hindi, English, Hinglish, or Gujarati from your first words.

  3. Confirm a one-time code

    Enter the code sent to your phone in the call or chat. Do not share it on this website.

  4. Give your name and consent

    Say what Bunty should call you. Choose separately whether to allow memory and call recording, with a clear explanation of each purpose.

  5. Answer one light question

    Share one simple preference, such as how you want to receive reminders.

The service line is not configured yet. No registration, form, or data collection happens here.

A QR code will be shown when the WhatsApp number is confirmed.
