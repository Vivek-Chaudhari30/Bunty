About Bunty

A personal assistant designed for people who already use a phone call or WhatsApp.

02

Built for everyday use.

Bunty is being built for people who want help with reminders, messages, calls, appointments, Google information, and food orders without learning a new app.

People should be able to speak or write in Hindi, English, Hinglish, or Gujarati. Before an external action, Bunty should read it back and wait for a clear confirmation. Money, bookings, and changes to personal data must be checked by code, not left to the AI alone.

The team is working across India, Boston, and the UK. Bunty is a prototype in development, and the public phone and WhatsApp lines are not configured yet.
