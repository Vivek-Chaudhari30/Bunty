About Bunty

A personal assistant you reach by phone call or WhatsApp.

Bunty is a personal assistant you reach by phone call or WhatsApp. You tell it what you need, and it takes care of it and confirms when it is done. We are a team of three, working across India, Boston and the UK.

Why Bunty

Most useful software asks you to download an app, make an account and learn a new screen. Many people do not want that, and some find it hard. Almost everyone can make a phone call or send a WhatsApp message. So Bunty works through those two things, and nothing else is needed.

You can speak or write to Bunty in Hindi, English, Hinglish or Gujarati. It remembers what you tell it, so you do not have to repeat yourself. Before it books something, calls someone or spends money, it reads the task back and waits for your yes. Payments are never automatic, because you approve each one in your own UPI app. When Bunty calls someone for you, it says it is an AI calling on your behalf.

Where we are

Bunty is in its prototype stage. We are testing with a small group of people, adding one task at a time, starting with reminders and WhatsApp, then calls, Google and food ordering.
