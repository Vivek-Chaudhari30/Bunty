Privacy policy

Draft for the Bunty prototype, written for people in India.

Draft · lawyer review required

This is a working draft about the intended handling of personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). It is not legal advice and does not claim that the prototype is compliant. An India-qualified lawyer must review it, and the responsible legal entity and contact details must be confirmed before launch.

1. Who is responsible

Data fiduciary / legal entity: [TBD]. Registered address: [TBD]. Privacy contact and grievance contact: [TBD]. The public phone and WhatsApp service lines are not configured at this time.

2. Data the service may process

When the service is available, it may process your phone number, name, language, messages and call transcripts, audio if recording is enabled, and details needed to carry out a task. If you connect another service, it may also process the data needed to use that connection.

This website has no contact form and does not ask you to enter personal information.

3. Separate purposes and consent

Bunty should explain each purpose in clear language and ask for a separate, affirmative choice where consent is required. A choice about one purpose must not be treated as consent to another.

  • Task handling: information needed to understand and carry out the request you made.
  • Memory: information you ask Bunty to remember for later requests.
  • Call recording: audio recording for the stated service purpose. Bunty should explain whether a call will be recorded and obtain consent before recording begins.
  • Connected services: access to a Google or other account, requested separately and limited to the task and access granted.

The service should record the consent choice, its purpose, and the time it was given. You should be able to withdraw a choice using a simple route that is no harder than giving it.

4. How data is used

Data is intended to help understand your request, remember details you chose to save, perform a confirmed task, and send you a result. Messages, bookings, calls, or other external actions must be read back and approved by you before code carries them out. The AI does not make the final decision about money, bookings, or changes to your data.

When Bunty calls another person for you, it must say that it is an AI calling on your behalf.

5. Providers and sharing

Some service providers may process information to support calls, messaging, speech, AI responses, or connected tools. Before launch, the responsible team must identify these providers, explain what each receives, confirm where data is processed, and document the applicable safeguards. Provider list and retention details: [TBD].

6. Retention and deletion

Retention periods are not set: [TBD]. The service should keep personal data only for a stated purpose and period, subject to applicable law. You may ask for deletion of your profile, memory, transcripts, recordings, and connected credentials, subject to any legal retention requirement. The service must confirm what was deleted and explain any information it must keep.

The intended request is “delete my data” in a call or WhatsApp chat. Those service channels are not available yet, so the request method and response time must be confirmed before this policy is used.

7. Security and children

The team should use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, limit access to authorised people, and explain how it will handle a personal data breach. Security contact and incident process: [TBD]. Rules for children and any required parent or guardian consent must be reviewed before the service is offered to children.

8. Your choices and complaints

You may ask to access or correct your information, withdraw consent, request deletion, or raise a grievance, subject to applicable law. Grievance contact, response period, and any required escalation details: [TBD]. These details must be supplied and checked by counsel before launch.

9. Changes to this draft

This draft is dated 22 October 2026. The final policy should state when it changes and how users will be told about material changes.
