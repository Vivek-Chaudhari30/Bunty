Privacy Policy

First draft for prototype stage. Subject to legal review.

Notice: This draft outlines how Bunty AI Assistant collects, uses, and protects personal information during our prototype testing phase. This document is a preliminary draft and must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel prior to commercial launch.

1. Information we collect

Bunty operates over phone calls and WhatsApp messaging. In providing this assistance, we process:

  • Contact details: Your mobile phone number used for WhatsApp messaging and voice calls, and your chosen display name.
  • Conversation and audio records: Transcripts of text conversations and audio recordings of telephone interactions with the assistant.
  • Assistant memory: Contextual facts you instruct Bunty to remember (such as requested reminders, preferred language, and appointment times) to avoid repetitive queries.
  • External account tokens: When you connect third-party integrations like Google Workspace, tokens are handled via dedicated credential executors without exposing master passwords to conversational models.

2. Explicit consent and timestamps

Data processing occurs solely upon your explicit consent. When you first interact with Bunty, your agreement to audio processing and conversational memory is logged with a verifiable timestamp. You may review or revoke your consent at any time.

3. Purpose of data use

We process your data strictly to execute requested assistant tasks:

  • Scheduling and dispatching reminders via WhatsApp or phone calls.
  • Transcribing and understanding verbal requests in Hindi, English, Hinglish, and Gujarati.
  • Contacting designated third parties or booking services upon your explicit verbal or text instruction.

4. Safety and human control principles

Our processing architecture enforces the following human-in-the-loop safeguards:

  • Explicit confirmation: Bunty reads back actions involving third parties, bookings, or expenses, and waits for a distinct confirmation ("yes") before initiating any action.
  • No automated payments: Bunty does not have direct access to your banking credentials. When a financial transaction is necessary, you approve a payment request directly in your UPI application using your private UPI PIN.
  • Caller identification: When calling external numbers on your behalf, Bunty explicitly announces that it is an artificial intelligence calling for you.
  • Treating external content as information: Inbound text from third-party emails, websites, or call recipients is treated as data for review, never as executable instructions for the assistant.

5. Data retention and deletion ("Delete my data")

During the prototype testing phase, data is retained for [RETENTION PERIOD placeholder: to be determined by legal review] or until you request deletion. You retain total ownership of your personal records. Saying "delete my data" or sending that request via WhatsApp or email permanently purges your conversations, contact profile, and memory records from our active stores.
