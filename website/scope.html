Scope of capabilities

What Bunty handles today, what is planned next, and our deliberate limits.

1. In testing now

Reminders

Setting time-based reminders via WhatsApp messages and voice calls. Confirmation is sent to WhatsApp immediately and when the reminder triggers.

WhatsApp messaging

Natural language conversations in Hindi, English, Hinglish, and Gujarati. Preserves memory across calls and chats so you do not repeat yourself.

2. Coming next

Phone calls

Dialing contacts or receiving incoming voice calls with real-time speech processing and natural voice responses.

Google Workspace (Gmail and Calendar)

Checking emails, drafting replies, and adding calendar appointments through verified Composio integrations.

Food orders and local bookings

Finding meals and booking clinic or salon visits by speaking directly to venues or utilizing verified ordering tools.

3. What Bunty does not do

No automatic money movement

Bunty cannot debit your account or auto-charge cards. You approve all payments directly inside your chosen UPI application using your private UPI PIN.

No obedience to third-party instructions

Incoming text from external websites, received emails, or what a third party says on a phone call is processed as information, never as executive instructions.

No undisclosed caller identity

Bunty always explicitly identifies itself as an AI assistant making a call on your behalf whenever speaking to outside recipients.

No unconsented data retention

Data use and audio logs require explicit consent, timestamped upon registration. Requesting data deletion purges your records permanently.
