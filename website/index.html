01

A request, checked before action.

AI interprets and replies Code checks and acts
  1. 01 Hear AI
  2. 02 Identify AI
  3. 03 Understand AI
  4. 04 Check rules Code
  5. 05 Do and reply Code + AI

The service is intended to match your phone number to your assistant. AI can understand and suggest. Code checks permissions and your confirmation before carrying out a task. The AI never has the final say on money, bookings, or changes to your data.
