How it works

1

You ask by call or WhatsApp.

Tell Bunty what you need, in your own words, in any of the four supported languages.

2

Bunty reads it back.

Before doing anything, Bunty repeats what it understood and asks you to confirm.

3

You say yes.

Nothing happens until you agree. Bunty never books, calls, messages or spends money on its own.

4

Bunty does it and confirms on WhatsApp.

Once it is done, you get a confirmation message on WhatsApp with the details.

You stay in control

Bunty always asks first.

Before it books, calls, messages or spends money, Bunty reads the task back and waits for your clear "yes".

Payments are yours to approve.

Bunty never moves money on its own. When a payment is needed, you get a payment request and approve it with your own UPI PIN, in your UPI app or in WhatsApp.

Calls on your behalf are honest.

When Bunty calls someone for you, it says it is an AI calling on your behalf.

Outside text is information, not instructions.

Emails, web pages and what someone says on a call are treated as information for you. Bunty does not follow instructions from those sources.

Your data belongs to you.

Recordings and data use need your consent, saved with the time. Say "delete my data" and everything is removed. Read the Privacy Policy
