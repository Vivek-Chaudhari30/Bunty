Notice: These terms govern participation in the prototype testing phase of Bunty AI Assistant. This draft must be reviewed by legal counsel before commercial availability.

1. Prototype nature of the service

Bunty is an experimental personal assistant operating on telephone voice and WhatsApp channels. Features are added iteratively. You acknowledge that capabilities may change, experience occasional downtime, or undergo modifications as system testing proceeds.

2. Permitted use and guardian responsibility

You agree to use Bunty for legitimate personal and administrative tasks. You are designated as the guardian of your assistant instance. You agree not to use the service to dispatch unlawful communications, harass recipients, generate fraudulent calls, or violate telecommunication guidelines.

3. Task confirmation and user agency

Bunty is programmed to read back tasks involving third parties, bookings, or outbound communications and require an explicit "yes" before proceeding. You remain solely responsible for the instructions you provide and confirm. If Bunty's read-back does not accurately reflect your desire, you must refuse confirmation or correct the details.

4. Payments and financial authorizations

Bunty does not hold financial custodial authority and cannot independently initiate monetary transfers. All payments require individual user authorization via standard UPI payment requests in your own UPI application using your private UPI PIN.

5. Automated calling disclosure

Whenever Bunty dials a clinic, merchant, or third party on your behalf, it automatically identifies itself as an AI assistant acting for you. You agree not to attempt to circumvent or disable this automated caller identification.

6. Third-party integrations

Access to external tools (such as Google Calendar, Gmail, Zomato, or Swiggy) requires separate authorization. Bunty is not responsible for third-party platform downtime, policy changes, or service interruptions outside our direct control.

7. Termination and data removal

You may discontinue your testing participation at any time. Simply state "delete my data" in chat or call to trigger the removal of your conversation history and contextual profile from our active systems.
