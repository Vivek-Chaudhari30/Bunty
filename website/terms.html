Terms of service

Draft terms for the Bunty prototype in India.

Draft · lawyer review required

This draft describes intended rules for using Bunty. It must be reviewed by an India-qualified lawyer before anyone is asked to accept it. Legal entity, contact details, pricing, and the public service line are not confirmed.

1. Prototype service

Bunty is a prototype personal assistant intended to work through phone calls and WhatsApp. Features may be incomplete or unavailable. The public service is not connected yet, and pricing is [TBD].

2. Your instructions and approval

Check the details Bunty reads back to you. Do not confirm a request if the recipient, message, amount, time, or other detail is wrong. Code must verify your clear confirmation before sending a message, making a call, booking something, or carrying out another external action.

AI-generated suggestions can be wrong. Do not rely on Bunty as a substitute for professional advice or emergency services.

3. Calls made for you

When Bunty calls another person on your behalf, it must say it is an AI assistant calling for you. Do not use the service to mislead, threaten, harass, or make unlawful calls.

4. Payments

Bunty must not ask for or handle your UPI PIN, card security code, or banking password. It may show or send a payment request only after telling you the amount and purpose. You decide whether to approve the payment inside your own payment app.

5. Personal data and consent

Use of personal data, memory, call recordings, and connected accounts should be explained by purpose and require the applicable consent. See the Privacy policy draft. Do not share another person’s sensitive information unless you have a lawful reason to do so.

You may request deletion of your data. The final service must provide a working request route and explain any legal reason for retaining specific information.

6. Third-party services

Some tasks may depend on phone, messaging, AI, or other providers. Their own terms may also apply. The final list of providers and any third-party charges must be stated before the service is offered.

7. Availability and changes

Prototype features may change or be stopped. Before public use, the responsible entity must state the service terms, support route, complaint process, and how it will notify users of material changes.
